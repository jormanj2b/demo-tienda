What cookies are

Cookies and similar tools, like your browser’s local storage, save small bits of data on your device. Some keep the site working. Others, like analytics or advertising, only load if you say yes.

Analytics: we count visits in aggregate with GoatCounter. It sets no cookies and stores no identifier on your device, so it isn’t in this table and doesn’t need your consent. Marketing: we don’t run ads or third-party content that sets cookies. If analytics or marketing cookies are ever added, they won’t load until you accept them, and this page will list what they are, who sets them and how long they last.
