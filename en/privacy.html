Privacy

Slow Clay is a fictional business. This site exists to show web design and development work.

What we collect

Nothing. The forms in this demo check what you type inside your own browser and don’t send it to any server. The checkout is a test: it never asks for card details and nothing is charged.

Local storage and cookies

This site uses no tracking cookies. We count visits with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies or identifiers (see the cookie policy). Some demos keep data in your browser’s local storage, such as a cart, so it survives a reload. You can clear it in your browser settings.
